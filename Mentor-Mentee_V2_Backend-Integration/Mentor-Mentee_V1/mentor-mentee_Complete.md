# 1. Guidelines

Mentoring is a proactive relation between Mentor and Mentee. Mentors are student centric teachers-friends who support and help young minds in their transitory phases and challenging times. It is based on mutual trust and respect. It has been introduced for obtaining a holistic assessment of the learners' performance and providing space for growth and development. Mentors apply their guidance, experience and expertise in promoting their mentees professionally and personally, through interpersonal engagement.

They build bridges among stakeholders as follows:

- Director & Stakeholders
- Students & Teachers
- Teachers & Parents
- Teachers & other related committees & counselors

## 1.1 Objectives

- Provisioning a comprehensive Individual Student Record.
- Creating opportunities amongst college groups & communities for bonding.
- Maintaining database of student performance, attendance details & drop outs.
- Providing feedback to parents and communicating with them on a timely basis.
- Providing assistance in developing responsibilities & values among students.

## 1.2 Expected Outcomes

- A Healthy Learning Environment. Vibrant Peer Group Exchanges.
- Creation of a positive communication channel among Principal, Staff & Students.
- Enhancing a feeling of belongingness among students.
- Self-confident and enthusiastic student's community.
- Better attendance, lesser dropouts and good p logical health of students.
- Well informed Parents.
- Improved performance.

## 1.3 Roles and Responsibilities of Mentors

- Conduct an orientation meeting for the fresher's by the first month of the semester to apprise them of the process & prepare an orientation report.
- Select a spokesperson/representative for the group.
- Collect data of mentees & maintain a file (profiling the students).
- Identify their areas of marked improvement in certain subjects and refer the same to respective Head of Departments.
- Track the attendance record & identify drop outs, if any, and prepare a review reprint.
- Allow & guide Peer Mentoring.
- Refer distressed or disturbed cases to the Counselling Committee through the Mentors & Class Coordinator's (CC's).
- Motivate them to take part in activities that can improve their performance.
- Compile and analyze their progress through a performance & discuss with them in detail one on one.
- Identify reasons for poor performance & attach a note (illness, domestic disturbances, personal obstacles etc.).
- Meet them twice in a month and record the same as a Review Report, to be sent to the Mentor-Mentee Committee.
- A total of four reports to be prepared (One Orientation & three Monthly Reports).
- Ensure holistic well-being of mentees.

## 1.4 Roles and Responsibilities of Mentees

- Provide all the necessary data with relevant documents (Previous Academic Records, documents for participation in extra/co-curricular activities, academic advancement and any other achievement).
- Know what you want and ask for what you need.
- Identify special goals that you want to achieve and discuss them clearly with your mentor.
- Seek information regarding resources that you need from various sources.
- Conduct Peer Mentoring to help your mentor.
- Have a positive attitude.
- Accept feedback respectfully, cordially & discuss future course of action.
- Attend mentor meetings regularly & punctually.
- Provide feedback and communicate healthy developments in you as Interaction is the Key to a successful mentor-mentee relationship.

## 1.5 Parameters for Mentee Assessment

Assessment by Mentors is a continually evolving process. It should be engaging and student centric Assessment rather than evaluation, supporting student initiatives to help them in improving their performance. It focuses on certain criteria for grading the mentee.

The Mentee:

1. Is Punctual.
2. Undertakes Responsibilities—Like Spokesperson/Leader/Peer Mentor or any other.
3. Engages in Quality Voluntary Interaction frequently/voluntarily participates in Quality Interaction Sessions.
4. Undertakes Supportive/Positive Roles.
5. Employs Personal Development Strategies, thereby seeking suggestions, advice and is Prompt in following instructions.
6. Develops Team Spirit & Team building initiatives.
7. Cultivates commitment and takes responsibility for his/her behavior.
8. Has innovative & creative interest which he/she exhibit in extra-curricular activities.
9. Demonstrates healthy Interpersonal Skills.

**Note:** Each above mentioned criteria has equal weightage of value 1 and the scores are calculated on 10 point scale (i.e. I to 10).

|---|---|---|

### Additional Mentor-Mentee Guidelines

The Mentor-Mentee interface will provide prospect to Mentor so as to document the journey of a student's life. It is a gateway for the mentee to bring his/her progress into the bigger sphere.

1. Mentor shall keep mentees informed about various rules and regulations to be followed by the students and keep them informed of various important announcements.
2. Dissemination of all the information/announcements to the students shall be made through the Mentors also, apart from usual procedures followed at the institutional/departmental level for this purpose.
3. Mentor shall try to inculcate discipline and etiquettes in his/her mentees to make them excellent student.
4. Mentor shall create helpful atmosphere that his/her mentees feel comfortable to interact with him/her for the solution of their problems/difficulties.
5. Mentor shall try to resolve the problem, if any of a mentee, to the best of his/her abilities report about any unresolved issue to their superiors. The details of the problem and the suggested measures must be properly documented.
6. Departmental Heads shall have frequent meetings with the Mentors of the respective departments to review and have feedbacks of their mentorship activation and provide appropriate guidance and corrective measures, as per need.
7. Director of the Institution along with concerned HoDs shall meet the Mentors.
8. Efforts shall be made to hold Parents Mentors (Teachers) meetings. Such meetings should preferably be planned on Saturdays and concerned parents should be informed well in advance by the respective mentors with prior information to their HoDs. Feedback and suggestions should be recorded, reviewed and should be brought to the notice of the HoDs/Director.
9. Mentors may recommend through proper channels, reward for a mentee for making extraordinary achievement at the University level or at any renowned forum outside the Institute, be it in academics, sports or co-curricular activities.
10. A Mentor, whose efforts enable any of his/her mentees in his/her overall development including extraordinary achievement at the University/National/International level, may also be recognized and rewarded.

## 1.7 Special Interests and Hobbies

Co-Curricular Activities are activities that supplement syllabi learning and enhance performance outcomes. They supplement and strengthen classroom learning. Activities like Certificate Courses in different disciplines, College sponsored Sporting Activities like Yoga Courses, Club Activities from different discipline related clubs like Science Club, Technical Club, Social Club, Sports Club etc., are all grouped under Co-Curricular Activities.

Extra-Curricular are activities that are required, but not directly related to the curriculum of the college. However, they enhance the personality, well-being and confidence of learners while ingraining codes of discipline and schemes like NSS, NCC, RED CROSS, SCOUTS and GUIDES come under this category.
